# Bloob

A private film and series app for a rooted LG webOS TV: browse, pick a
version, play it through the owner's AllDebrid account with Bloob's own
engine on the TV's hardware decoders.

- **Start here:** [HANDOVER.md](HANDOVER.md): what it is, how to build and
  ship it, the rules, and what is unfinished.
- Full history: [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md)
- The app (C++): [native/](native/README.md)
- The service that runs beside it on the TV (Node): [tv-app/service/](tv-app/service/)
- Latest TV package: [native/artifacts/com.muhib.bloob_4.1.1_arm.ipk](native/artifacts/com.muhib.bloob_4.1.1_arm.ipk)

Extracted reference-app files, decompiler output, downloaded tools and
account data stay local and are excluded from this repository.
